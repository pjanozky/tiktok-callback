Privacy Policy – YT Live

YT Live is a personal, non-commercial analytics tool used only by its owner to view statistics of the owner's own TikTok account.

Data accessed: basic profile info, follower/like/video counts and the owner's own video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
